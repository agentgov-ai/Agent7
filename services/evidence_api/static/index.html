Enforcement Mode

-

The effective mode is the stricter of what the SDK sends and the dashboard override, so this can tighten a system's posture but never switch enforcement off. A change applies to the governed app's next action, with no restart.

Kill Switches

-

A kill switch outranks every other rule. Enabling one denies its capabilities on the next call, without redeploying the governed app.
